Identical starts, distinct algorithms
Uniform reservoir stagnation or a numerical adapted expansion from a 50 → 100 → 200 seed chain. Each target starts on 200 cells. Direct Newton solves the unshifted coupled steady residual with an exact AD Jacobian and custom PySolverView initial-step control; implicit Euler adds the physical storage derivative divided by its pseudo-timestep.
Three independent outcomes
Native solver termination is retained as evidence. A fresh audit checks the original steady spatial residual, boundary constraints and global conservation. A separate branch diagnostic checks the intended internal shock and analytical position. A small update or backend success alone establishes none of the latter checks.
Single-trajectory timings
These are robustness experiments, not repeated speed benchmarks. Compilation/cache state, concurrent CPU work and dense direct versus banded implicit linear algebra differ. Seed preparation, solve, compilation and audit costs are retained where measured. No speed ranking is inferred from one trajectory.
All failures preserved
Singular initial Jacobians, no-flow states, invalid domain trials, rejected steps and finite iteration limits remain visible. A successful fallback or pseudo-transient prefix is never substituted for direct Newton.
