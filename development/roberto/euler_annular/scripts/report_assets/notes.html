CFL and Newton-cap findings

The selected steady policy is initial CFL 0.2, fixed accepted-step multiplier 1.2, failure multiplier 0.5, maximum CFL 10⁶, and at most eight Newton corrections. Both native inner criteria remain 10⁻¹⁰, with 12 retries. The 400-cell shock35 initial timestep is approximately 7.055338 × 10⁻⁸ s; dimensional timesteps change with mesh and EOS wave speeds. This is a measured conservative starting point, not a universal optimum.

The initial strict five-correction screen could not accept the first cold step: an implicit residual at roundoff did not make the fifth correction satisfy the separate small-update test. A separately labeled residual-verified five-cap variant accepted only admissible candidates already meeting the same 10⁻¹⁰ implicit-residual target. It was experimental; the current campaign uses eight corrections and both strict tests. Twelve provided little additional coarse-grid work benefit.

The native work-threshold policy changes when the cap changes: at a cap of five, a successful four/five-correction step holds CFL. That policy remained unconverged after 6,000 coarse-grid updates in the residual-verified screen. The fixed-growth alternatives grow after every accepted step. Growth 1.4 reduced accepted updates but increased retries/backtracking; shrink 0.8 needed many more failed attempts, while shrink 0.25 prolonged small-step marching.

A shock residual plateau can accompany a moving compression front. In the five-cap candidate MUSCL experiment, the gradient-based position proxy moved from about 0.100 m initially to 0.078 m at update 1,000, 0.053 m at update 3,000 and the reference 0.035 m near the end, with a temporary overshoot. This proxy is not unique shock tracking in a general transient. Monotonic steady residual reduction was not imposed.

Physical interpretation and unresolved scope

The acoustic eigenvalues depend on meridional velocity, but this does not mean swirl is irrelevant to choking or shocks. Swirl changes the EOS state, centrifugal forcing, effective sonic compatibility and shock-map states. Total Mach exceeding one is distinct from meridional Mach exceeding one.

Constant-width radial inflow can accelerate to a sonic exit, but cannot cross smoothly through an internal sonic point on the selected monotone radial geometry. The general sonic condition and its derivation are in the theory page. A constant-width bent NURBS midline with an internal radius minimum, or a variable-height purely radial throat, would define a different benchmark. That internal-transonic geometry remains unselected; no claimed verification silently substitutes one.

A possible constant-width proposal is $x(\xi)=0.20\xi$ m, $r(\xi)=0.10+0.40(\xi-1/2)^2$ m and $b=0.005$ m, represented by a unit-weight quadratic curve with control points $(0,0.20),(0.10,0),(0.20,0.20)$ m. The represented radius stays positive despite the middle control ordinate being zero. It bends inward and outward and is not a purely radial inflow. This is a deferred proposal, not a tested fixture.

Outward flow with increasing area and radius accelerates when meridionally supersonic on a smooth isentropic branch. The selected diffusion interpretations are total-supersonic but meridional-subsonic swirl-dominated flow, and a shock followed by subsonic diffusion. These are not a resolved rotating-detonation simulation. Reverse flow, automatic boundary switching, separation, heat/friction closures, nonclassical two-phase waves and chemistry remain outside the verified model.

Corrections carried forward from the original physics review

The density chain rule is $\rho'=\rho_p p'+\rho_h h'$, with $\rho_h=-\rho G/a^2$; it contains no extra velocity multiplier. If wall heat flux is in W/m², the steady energy relation is $\rho v_m h_0'=2q_w/b$. The exact stagnation identity is $dp_0=\rho_0(dh_0-T_0\,ds)$, giving $dp_0=-\rho_0T_0\,ds$ for adiabatic flow. The incompressible relation $p_0-p=\rho v^2/2$ is not an exact compressible reference, and constant flow angle is not generally preserved in compressible variable-density flow.

The sibling NozzleX review supplied geometry/physics provenance, not a validation oracle or runtime dependency. The reviewed ODE omitted density from the enthalpy derivative coefficient and the mass-flux factor from its wall-heating term. Independent conservation-law references were therefore used; no sibling checkout was changed. The acoustic timestep uses the largest speed $|v_m|+a$, not the characteristic tending to zero at a sonic point. Implicit linear stability alone proves neither nonlinear convergence nor admissibility. MUSCL dependencies require radius-two Jacobian coloring rather than an assumed block-tridiagonal stencil.

Accuracy, robustness and supporting evidence

The results table gives every achieved deviation beside the required limit. Smooth cases require max absolute Cp deviation below 0.001 Cp units and all four fixed-scale conservative L₁ deviations below 0.001. These are not percentages relative to possibly zero reference quantities. Shock-location passes do not certify pointwise accuracy or absence of overshoot. The constant shock70 local pressure excess remains about 26.524% over the analytical maximum in its audited shock window.

Both reconstructions converged on the graded radial mesh, while constant reconstruction still missed its Cp target. Earlier separate physical-time contact verification reached 0.00125 s with a 0.1 m pulse displacement and decreasing density error; accepted-step backward-Euler budget defects were below 2.5 × 10⁻¹⁵. Those physical-time tests were retained rather than rerun with a steady CFL controller, and they do not establish temporal order or exact nonreflecting boundaries.

The inherited characteristic entropy width 10⁻⁶ failed both cold-start shock35 reconstructions at 200 cells. An interrupted 400-cell attempt has no claimed result. Independent dense/colored AD agreement near 10⁻¹⁶ and banded/dense direction agreement near 10⁻¹⁰ excluded a structural assembly error in the diagnosed state. Severe sonic-regularization curvature defeated admissible Armijo trials. The selected width is 0.1 for both reconstructions, with unchanged tolerances and no hidden reference seed. Rusanov remains an explicit diagnostic, not a fallback. No unconditional robustness or nonlinear entropy-stability proof is claimed.

Historical implementation evidence includes an 81-check combined annular/shared suite, followed by 52 affected physics/adapter/FVM checks after conservative-recovery fixes; those suites overlap and must not be added. The focused nozzle suite passed 37 checks and six extraction comparisons were bitwise equal. All five legacy steady nozzle roots converged, but the documented sonic-limit accuracy check still failed (Mach L₁ 0.0311768 against 0.03). Three Sod snapshots passed, with a maximum normalized budget defect about 1.90 × 10⁻¹⁶. These are dated regression records, not new test runs performed during report cleanup.

Current saved-state audits separately check original spatial equations, all boundary rows, admissibility, branch selection, conservation, reference projection and array reproduction.
