Local equation residuals

R1–R4 measure whether individual cells satisfy mass, meridional momentum, angular momentum and energy. Each signed cell flux imbalance includes its discrete source, is scaled by fixed physical references, and enters a volume-weighted RMS. Squaring prevents cancellation between cells. Dividing each equation norm by its initial norm gives the relative curves, starting at one; the target is 10−9 for every active equation. The top-left R is their maximum.

Global conservation defects

These compare outlet flux minus inlet flux minus all integrated sources. They are divided by fixed reference fluxes, not initial defects, so they need not start at one. Signed cell defects can cancel in this sum: two cells with +1 and −1 give zero global imbalance although neither cell satisfies its equation. Global balance and local residual convergence are complementary checks; neither replaces the other. The separate global target is 10−8.

Metric definitions and accuracy reassessment

Spatial RMS: RMS of the original flux-difference-minus-source rows scaled by Vᵢ Uv,ref aref/L. Boundary maximum: the separate maximum of all eight scaled algebraic constraints. Conservation: (Fout − Fin − ΣSᵢ)/(AinarefUv,ref). Every active equation requires nine orders of reduction, with separate absolute, boundary and global safeguards of 1e-8.

Uv,ref = (ρref, ρrefaref, ρrefrinaref, ρrefaref²). Transported-flux spreads use separately documented nonzero normalizations; they are not the same metric as the fixed-scale integrated balance.

Smooth finest-grid targets are the meridional-length mean |delta Cp| ≤ 0.01 and all four conservative L1 errors < 0.001. Maximum |delta Cp| remains supplementary. Current accuracy uses the actual fields returned by the new-policy solves; archived assessments remain separate. The shock target is location error ≤ 2 cells. Accuracy discrepancies and local shock overshoots are retained. Coarse-to-fine initialization uses only converged numerical fields; no analytical reference is used as a numerical initial guess.
