01 / COMMON PHYSICS

Primitive variables and conservative Euler balances

The solver stores $U=(p,u,h)^\mathsf{T}$. Pressure and static enthalpy determine the perfect-gas thermodynamic state. The calorically perfect-gas enthalpy reference used here is $h=c_pT$, with $\gamma=1.4$ for the air backend.

$$ \rho=\frac{\gamma p}{(\gamma-1)h},\qquad a=\sqrt{(\gamma-1)h},\qquad M=\frac{u}{a}. $$

Let $H=h+u^2/2$ denote total enthalpy. The conservative state and physical Euler flux are

$$ Q(U)=\begin{bmatrix}\rho\\ \rho u\\ \rho H-p\end{bmatrix}, \qquad F(U)=\begin{bmatrix}\rho u\\ \rho u^2+p\\ \rho uH\end{bmatrix}. $$

The quasi-one-dimensional area source acts in momentum. For the inviscid, adiabatic nozzle used here, its integrated cell form is $V_iS_i$, with $S_i=(0,p_i A_i'/A_i,0)^\mathsf{T}$. The face-consistent area discretization, corrected FVS flux and ordinary MUSCL Van Leer limiter are imported unchanged.

$$ \begin{aligned} R_i^{\mathrm{BE}}={}&V_i[Q(U_i^{n+1})-Q(U_i^n)]\\ &+\Delta t\,[A_{i+1/2}\widehat F_{i+1/2} -A_{i-1/2}\widehat F_{i-1/2}]\\ &-\Delta t\,V_iS_i. \end{aligned} $$

Ghost states are algebraic boundary values and carry no independent storage. Interior face-flux formulas are shared by both approaches, although different ghosts naturally alter near-outlet reconstruction values. The inlet law is identical in both approaches and enforces reservoir total enthalpy and entropy through the existing compatible inlet construction.

02 / CURRENT OUTLET

A Mach-selected pressure/extrapolation blend

Use $i$ for the last physical cell, $i-1$ for the preceding physical cell and $g$ for the outlet ghost. The current outlet first computes area-weighted density and mass-flux extrapolations. The extrapolated density below is used in the ghost-velocity formula.

$$ \rho_e=\operatorname{clip}\!\left( \frac{2\rho_iA_i-\rho_{i-1}A_{i-1}}{A_g}, 10^{-3},10^4\right), $$
$$ u_g=\frac{2\rho_i u_iA_i-\rho_{i-1}u_{i-1}A_{i-1}} {\rho_e A_g}. $$

Static enthalpy is extrapolated. Static pressure blends the requested backpressure with extrapolation according to the last interior Mach number. The source's EOS-domain bounds on extrapolated pressure and enthalpy are retained.

$$ w(M_i)=\frac12\left[1+\tanh\left(\frac{1-|M_i|}{0.004}\right)\right], $$
$$ \begin{aligned} p_e&=\operatorname{clip}(2p_i-p_{i-1},p_{\min},p_{\max}),\\ p_g&=w(M_i)p_b+[1-w(M_i)]p_e,\\ h_g&=\operatorname{clip}(2h_i-h_{i-1},h_{\min},h_{\max}). \end{aligned} $$

The ghost thermodynamics are subsequently evaluated from $(h_g,p_g)$. At the adapted exit $M_i\simeq2.19$, $w$ is numerically zero. The prescribed pressure then supplies no forcing, so the unchanged adapted field can remain a steady root at a higher requested backpressure.

This is a limitation of a decision based only on the instantaneous interior Mach number. Infinitesimal acoustic characteristics all leave a supersonic outlet, but a sufficiently strong finite compression can move upstream. The new construction accounts for that finite-wave possibility.

03 / NEW PRESSURE-WAVE OUTLET

Choose the boundary trace using the nonlinear wave curve

The new law constructs a state compatible with prescribed pressure $p_b$ and the incoming acoustic family of the interior state. This is a partial pressure-boundary Riemann construction, rather than an arbitrary assignment of a complete exterior state. Density and velocity follow the entropy-admissible compression or expansion relation.

$$ q=\frac{p_b}{p_i},\qquad \beta=\frac{\gamma-1}{\gamma+1},\qquad A_i^{\mathrm{wave}}=\frac{2}{(\gamma+1)\rho_i}. $$

Compression: $p_b>p_i$

The shock curve gives the pressure-compatible state and its shock speed in the fixed frame:

$$ \rho_* =\rho_i\frac{q+\beta}{\beta q+1}, $$
$$ u_* =u_i-(p_b-p_i) \sqrt{\frac{A_i^{\mathrm{wave}}}{p_b+\beta p_i}}, $$
$$ S=u_i-a_i\sqrt{ \frac{\gamma+1}{2\gamma}q+\frac{\gamma-1}{2\gamma}}. $$

If $S<0$, the compression enters the right boundary and the boundary trace is the compatible compressed state. If $S\ge0$, the wave is outgoing and source-style extrapolation is retained. At zero amplitude the two constructions meet at the interior state.

Expansion: $p_b<p_i$

The expansion preserves interior entropy and the outgoing acoustic invariant:

$$ \begin{aligned} \rho_*&=\rho_i q^{1/\gamma},\\ a_*&=a_i q^{(\gamma-1)/(2\gamma)},\\ u_*&=u_i+\frac{2(a_i-a_*)}{\gamma-1}. \end{aligned} $$
$$ S_{\mathrm{head}}=u_i-a_i,\qquad S_{\mathrm{tail}}=u_*-a_*. $$

Sample the fan at zero normal wave speed. If its head is outgoing, use extrapolation. If the entire fan enters, use the prescribed-pressure state. If the fan straddles the boundary, use the sonic trace:

$$ a_s=u_s=\frac{(\gamma-1)u_i+2a_i}{\gamma+1}, $$
$$ \rho_s=\rho_i\left(\frac{a_s}{a_i}\right)^{2/(\gamma-1)}, \qquad p_s=p_i\left(\frac{a_s}{a_i}\right)^{2\gamma/(\gamma-1)}. $$

Every selected state uses consistent enthalpy $h=\gamma p/[(\gamma-1)\rho]$. The law preserves legitimate outgoing pressure independence when no incoming wave exists.

04 / ONE-WAY OUTFLOW WALL TREATMENT

Handle a demanded reverse boundary velocity explicitly

During steady convergence, the compatible pressure-wave state can demand $u_*<0$ while the interior velocity is still nonnegative. The working outlet then uses the local reflecting-wall state obtained by solving the compression curve for zero trace velocity.

$$ C=\frac{\gamma(\gamma+1)}{2}M_i^2,\qquad q_w=1+\frac C2+\frac12\sqrt{C^2+4C(1+\beta)}. $$
$$ p_w=p_iq_w,\qquad \rho_w=\rho_i\frac{q_w+\beta}{\beta q_w+1},\qquad u_w=0. $$
$$ F_w=\begin{bmatrix}0\\p_w\\0\end{bmatrix}. $$

This is an explicit one-way outflow treatment for steady iteration. It supplies no arbitrary exterior entropy or temperature and is not a general exterior-reservoir model for physical backflow. Actual reverse interior flow remains outside this prototype. Final verification requires the wall mode to be inactive, positive exit velocity and agreement with the requested pressure.

05 / DISCRETE BOUNDARY ENFORCEMENT

Use the sampled incoming trace on the last face

For an incoming wave, sonic trace or wall state, the final boundary face uses the physical flux of the selected trace. An outgoing boundary retains the existing FVS flux. Supplying a boundary trace through an averaged-characteristic face split alone would not ensure that the incoming nonlinear wave is represented in the boundary flux.

$$ \widehat F_{\mathrm{out}}= \begin{cases} F(U_{\mathrm{trace}}),&\text{incoming wave or sonic trace},\\ F_w,&\text{one-way wall state},\\ \widehat F_{\mathrm{FVS,out}},&\text{outgoing wave}. \end{cases} $$

Only the last face is replaced explicitly. The common finite-volume residual receives the corresponding conservative correction in the last physical cell:

$$ \begin{aligned} R_{N-2}^{\mathrm{new}}={}&R_{N-2}^{\mathrm{common}}\\ &+\Delta t\,A_{N-3/2} \left(\widehat F_{\mathrm{out}}-\widehat F_{\mathrm{FVS,out}}\right). \end{aligned} $$

The same final-face correction is used in the mass-flux monitor. Jacobians include the selected boundary map and flux derivative. Colored/dense AD agreement and the telescoping mass balance are independently checked. Branch changes remain piecewise-defined; no smooth derivative through every shock-entry threshold is claimed.

06 / IMPLICIT UPDATE AND INNER CORRECTIONS

Inner Newton corrections for the backward-Euler step

At each pseudo-time update, the old physical state is fixed and the new interior primitive state is the unknown. A correction solves the differentiated backward-Euler equations, including the boundary response:

$$ J_{\mathrm{BE}}(U^{(k)})\,\delta U^{(k)} =-R_{\mathrm{BE}}(U^{(k)};U^n,\Delta t), $$
$$ U^{(k+1)}=U^{(k)}+\delta U^{(k)}. $$

The compared configurations use colored AD, a banded linear solve, an inner Newton correction cap of eight and an inner relative-update tolerance of $10^{-10}$. The cap is a maximum, not proof that each nonlinear timestep equation is solved exactly. The relative-update criterion uses cellwise pressure magnitude, the global velocity magnitude plus 1 m/s, and the global enthalpy magnitude as scales.

The independently audited steady stopping quantity is a fixed-scale spatial residual. With $t_0=\Delta x/a_0$, the balance scales are built from reservoir quantities and physical-cell volumes:

$$ R_{\mathrm{ref},i}=\frac{V_i\rho_0}{t_0} \begin{bmatrix}1\\a_0\\h_0\end{bmatrix}, \qquad r_{\mathrm{RMS}}= \sqrt{\frac1{N_{\mathrm{dof}}} \sum_k\left(\frac{R_{\mathrm{ss},k}}{R_{\mathrm{ref},k}}\right)^2}. $$

The outer target is $r_{\mathrm{RMS}}\le10^{-8}$, with at most 5000 accepted updates. The source SSR is recorded only as a diagnostic; it does not control the timestep ramp. The calculations verify steady endpoints; they do not establish physical-time startup accuracy.

07 / TIMESTEP CONTROLLER AND ACTUAL CFL

Fixed CFL growth with verified implicit steps

We transfer the conservative recommendation from euler_annular/cfl_investigation/REPORT.md: initial CFL 0.2, accepted growth 1.2, failed-trial shrink 0.5, maximum CFL $10^6$, eight inner Newton corrections and up to twelve retries. This is a fixed-growth controller, without SER or work-based thresholds. The production nozzle Newton kernel retains full corrections without the annular inner line search.

$$ \lambda^n=\max_{i\in\mathrm{physical}}(|u_i^n|+a_i^n),\qquad \Delta t^n=C_n\frac{\Delta x}{\lambda^n},\qquad C_0=0.2. $$
$$ C_{\mathrm{trial}}\leftarrow 0.5 C_{\mathrm{trial}}\quad\text{after rejection},\qquad C_{n+1}=\min(1.2 C_{\mathrm{accepted},n},10^6)\quad\text{after acceptance}. $$

Relative Newton update and backward-Euler residual must both be below $10^{-10}$. The residual is normalized by fixed reservoir conservative storage scales, not by a vanishing initial momentum:

$$ D_i=V_i\rho_0\begin{bmatrix}1\\a_0\\h_0\end{bmatrix},\qquad \sqrt{\frac1{N_{\mathrm{dof}}}\sum_k(R_{\mathrm{BE},k}/D_k)^2}\le10^{-10}. $$

A cap-limited or invalid inner solve is rejected. The completed full state, including algebraic ghosts, must also satisfy the same increment bounds in both methods:

$$ \max_j\frac{|\Delta p_j|}{p_j^n+\epsilon}\le0.2,\qquad \max_j\frac{|\Delta h_j|}{|h_j^n|+\epsilon}\le0.1,\qquad \max_j\frac{|\Delta u_j|}{|u_j^n|+a_j^n+\epsilon}\le0.2. $$
$$ \mathrm{CFL}_{\mathrm{accepted},n}=\frac{\Delta t_{\mathrm{accepted},n}\lambda^n}{\Delta x}. $$

The old physical state supplies the wave speed. The initial history index has no accepted timestep or actual CFL. This policy changes iteration trajectories relative to the historical SER/one-correction experiment; only the newly rerun matched comparisons are presented. It is a steady pseudo-time study, not physical-time startup validation.

08 / VERIFICATION AND LIMITS

Check the original equations and the intended physical branch

A verified endpoint must satisfy the spatial RMS target, have a captured internal supersonic-to-subsonic transition and positive subsonic exit velocity, match the prescribed outlet pressure to relative error below $10^{-6}$, and leave the wall mode inactive. Relative inlet/outlet mass imbalance must be below $10^{-4}$. Normalized mean errors in pressure, density and Mach are assessed separately, each against 0.03.

The first results page uses identical uniform stagnation interior states $(p_0,0,h_0)$; the second uses identical adapted interior states computed by the source implicit scheme. Ghosts are completed by each outlet law. The adapted seed is generated numerically, without an analytical flowfield initializer. Analytical profiles are used only to define and check the cases. These two cases establish the tested high-backpressure behavior on one mesh. They do not establish a general-EOS closure, physical backflow accuracy, mesh convergence or robust behavior across every outgoing/incoming switch.
