01 / CASE AND PHYSICAL SETUP

Two shocked target pressures, matched initialization

Perfect-gas air enters a symmetric 0.2 m converging-diverging nozzle from a reservoir at 200 kPa and 300 K. The exit-to-throat area ratio is two. The target pressures place analytical shocks at 35% and 70% of the divergent section; those shock locations define references only. @@INITIAL_SETUP@@

The current outlet can preserve the pressure-insensitive supersonic branch. A small Euler residual alone therefore does not establish the intended shocked solution. Pressure, Mach number, conservation and the final outlet state are checked separately.

02 / CONTROLLED NUMERICAL METHOD

Same implicit solver, different outlet treatment

Both approaches reuse the same geometry, inlet law, conservative storage, source terms, interior flux formulas and limiter. The new outlet uses the physical Euler flux of a sampled incoming-wave trace on the last face. Outgoing flow retains the source FVS flux. Ghost values can naturally alter near-outlet MUSCL slopes and adjacent flux values.

The inner Newton correction cap is a maximum, not a statement that each nonlinear backward-Euler system is solved exactly. The independent stopping criterion is the fixed-scale spatial residual RMS ≤ 10−8. Each accepted step must satisfy relative Newton correction and fixed-storage-scaled implicit residual below 10−10. CFL grows after acceptance and shrinks after failure; no SER ramp is used.
