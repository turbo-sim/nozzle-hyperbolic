## What changed

- Added optional model-owned Roe data and a generic conservative Roe flux. No EOS or Euler variable indices enter the shared numerical layer.
- Nozzle and annular models use the existing PH calls, centered secant coefficients, grouped projectors and acoustic-only Harten correction. A C2 small-jump blend avoids division-amplified roundoff; invalid averages reject the trial without clipping or a hidden HLL fallback.
- Explicit Roe timesteps additionally bound reconstructed interface dissipation speeds. Existing fluxes and implicit CFL policy retain their definitions.
- The benchmark now treats flux as an explicit dimension in keys, bindings, resume identity, audits and offline report controls.

### Refinement and interpretation

Every recorded conservative L1 error decreases on refinement: **True**. Observed orders range from **0.56 to 0.72**, consistent with a shock/contact test using constant reconstruction. Different finite-CFL time integrators have different numerical diffusion; the smaller explicit-Euler error here does not establish a general accuracy ranking.

Coupled and segregated backward-Euler final fields agree within **5.06e-13** in normalized primitive variables. They solve the same implicit equations.

The exact reference uses 128-point Gauss quadrature per cell. The largest retained 64-versus-128 normalized L1 difference is **0.000103**. This is smaller than the reported discretization error but limits fine accuracy claims near discontinuities; it is a sensitivity estimate, not a rigorous quadrature bound.

Of those corrections, 1688 were spent on rejected trials. With the prescribed cap 10, median accepted CFL was **3.114**; 860 accepted steps stayed below CFL 5.

Pending update convergence is inferred from the verified simultaneous convergence rule; the actual update magnitudes were not logged. Another **140 status-3 full-trial failures** do not identify whether cell/trace admissibility, Roe-face validity or nonfinite residuals caused the rejection. These records do not establish limiter cycling or isolate a Roe-versus-FVS cause.

This is a numerical-work limitation under the matched controls, not a failed final root or a timing comparison.
