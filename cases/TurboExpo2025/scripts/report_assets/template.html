How to interpret the comparison

The historical plotting scripts refer to solution archives that were kept outside Git. Those original numerical profiles are unavailable in the repository. Experimental and reference CFD data are identified by their own sources; they are not relabeled as the paper's HEM solution.

Equilibrium HEM can differ from experiments with delayed phase change. A converged residual establishes the requested discrete root only when the physical branch, boundary and conservation checks also pass. It does not by itself establish experimental agreement or readiness to merge the branch.

Spatial method and steady integration
The reproduction uses the current shared finite-volume engine with piecewise-constant reconstruction. Rusanov and HLL are evaluated for the real-fluid cases; the perfect-gas verification also includes Roe. Backward Euler and the current Newton solve replace the publication's explicit pseudo-time march.
Numerical boundary traces are solved with the current model equations. The exact selected method, nonlinear settings, initial field, mesh, EOS and source fingerprints are saved for each attempt. Changes to thermodynamic closures, geometric integration, conservative flux geometry and outlet equations can change the discrete solution.
Residual and conservation checks
For physical cell $i$ and equation $k$, the original spatial residual is
$$r_{i,k}=\Phi_{i+1/2,k}-\Phi_{i-1/2,k}-S_{i,k}.$$
Fixed physical scales give
$$R_{2,k}=\left[\sum_i\frac{V_i}{\sum_jV_j}\left(\frac{t_{\mathrm{ref}}r_{i,k}}{V_iq_{\mathrm{ref},k}}\right)^2\right]^{1/2}.$$
Boundary equations, admissibility, physical regime and the numerical boundary-flux/source budgets are checked independently. Numerical face mass flux is the conserved quantity; cell $\rho u A$ can vary on a finite mesh.
