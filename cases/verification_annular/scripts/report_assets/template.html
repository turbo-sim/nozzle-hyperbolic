04 / MATHEMATICAL EQUIVALENCE

One physical model, two marching directions

Meridional arclength derivatives are denoted by primes.

01

Space marching

NozzleX specifies the complete inlet state and integrates the smooth steady ODE downstream with Dopri5. The inlet reservoir is 101.325 kPa and 300 K, total inlet Mach is 0.8, and the flow angle is 60°, giving meridional Mach 0.4.

02

Time marching to steady state

The hyperbolic solver prescribes inlet reservoir enthalpy, entropy and angle, with backpressure taken from NozzleX’s predicted outlet static pressure. Inlet Mach emerges from this subsonic boundary problem. The recorded FVM method is HLL with MUSCL van Leer and coupled backward-Euler pseudo-time marching.

03

Corrected reference balances

NozzleX’s continuity enthalpy coefficient is −ρG/a². Its enthalpy derivative energy row uses 2qw/(bρvm), and integrated fluid entropy includes wall heat. The steady ODE’s sonic singularity is meridional Mach one. These cases stay on the forward, subsonic meridional branch.

04

Imported closures

Both solvers call the same NozzleX wall-load function: zero or constant Fanning friction, Aungier wall/diffusion/curvature losses, and adiabatic or Reynolds-analogy heating. Diffusion and curvature losses act only meridionally; wall friction also removes angular momentum. Heating uses local stagnation temperature and wall friction alone.

Read the local error in physical units.

Each error is |FVM − NozzleX| at a saved cell center, scaled only to the unit shown on its axis. There is no interpolation of the reference for these comparisons. The log plot omits exact zero values instead of replacing them with an artificial floor. Global conservative errors use volume weights and fixed production scales; they compare point-sampled states, not exact reference volume averages.

Scope of the conclusion

Agreement verifies these shared reduced equations and discretizations for perfect-gas air. It does not independently validate the common empirical closures or resolve transverse pressure gradients, boundary layers and separation. Aungier’s entrance multiplier retains an attribution TODO, and its configured Reynolds-transition fields remain ineffective.
